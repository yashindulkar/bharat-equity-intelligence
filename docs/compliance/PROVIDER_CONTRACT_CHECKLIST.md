# Provider contract checklist

Obtain executed terms and clause references for: contracting entities/product/version; effective/renewal/termination dates; named household users and private research; venue/instruments/fields/history/delistings; publication timestamps/revisions; constituent/TRI and branding; delivery/SLA/support/cost; credentials and security; raw/cache retention; derived works/features; backtesting; model training; AI/subprocessors; backup/restore; fixtures; internal display; citation/attribution; redistribution prohibition; geography/cloud; breach/incident; audit; deletion schedule/certificate; post-termination evidence and reproducibility; liability and change notice. Record counsel/licensing reviewer, approval status/date, exceptions, and re-review date. Unknown clauses fail closed.
