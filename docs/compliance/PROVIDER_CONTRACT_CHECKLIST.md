# Provider contract checklist

Obtain executed terms and versioned clause references for: contracting entities; provider/product/version; agreement and policy-snapshot identity; effective, renewal, scheduled-expiry and early-termination dates/event IDs/amendments; named household users and private research; venue/instruments/fields/history/delistings; publication timestamps/revisions; constituent/TRI and branding; delivery/SLA/support/cost; credentials and security; raw/cache retention; derived works/features; backtesting; model training; AI/subprocessors; backup/restore; fixtures; internal display; citation/attribution; redistribution prohibition; explicit processing jurisdictions or evidenced worldwide scope; cloud; breach/incident; audit; category-specific deletion deadlines/certificates/verifiers for raw, backup, fixtures, derived data and audit evidence; permitted retained categories; post-termination reproduction; liability; and change notice.

Record reviewer, approval status/date, evidence availability, exceptions, and re-review date. Require a new snapshot and re-evaluation after agreement/policy changes. Unknown clauses, missing lifecycle records, non-category-specific deletion evidence and empty geography rights fail closed.
