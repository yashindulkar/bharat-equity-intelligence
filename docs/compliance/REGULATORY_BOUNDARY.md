# Regulatory Boundary

## Current boundary

**Assumption, not legal conclusion:** private, unpaid, household-only research; no publication, third-party access, social sharing, securities-specific push solicitation, public performance marketing, personalized order execution or broker trading. The system labels output research for review and never promises returns.

- [CERT-In directions under IT Act §70B](https://cert-in.org.in/Directions70B.jsp) require deployment-specific applicability review, not a blanket compliance claim.

Disclaimers do not determine IA/RA status. Personalized suitability/allocation output is legally consequential. Counsel must classify the exact operator, user relationship, consideration, communication and functionality before Phase 3 or any external/paid use.

## Hard expansion gates

Counsel, product and security approval is required before collecting suitability/portfolio data; third-party or paid access; public output; personalized notifications; external AI use; broker connectivity; or any order capability. Data licenses need separate approval.

## Required behavior

Research ranking remains analytically separate from suitability and portfolio proposals. User reviews evidence and manually places any order outside V1. Regulatory/data-rights statements are versioned; the product must never claim “SEBI compliant/registered” absent documented status.
