# ADR-0016: Initial universe and benchmark policy

- Status: Partially Accepted
- Date: 2026-07-13
- Revised: 2026-07-17

Nifty 200 is the intended prospective initial universe. Historical analysis requires licensed effective-dated membership and delisted coverage; current membership cannot be applied retrospectively. A licensed total-return benchmark product and methodology remain an open gate, so no benchmark is approved.

For history-gap relevance, intervals are closed. A universe-scoped gap is presumed relevant unless effective-dated membership evidence available by the evaluation cutoff proves non-membership across the entire overlap. Unknown membership, partial coverage and membership changes fail closed. Production historical-universe resolution remains deferred; Task 2 does not fabricate it.
