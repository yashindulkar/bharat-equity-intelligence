# Source precedence

**Proposal.** Precedence is field- and purpose-specific, versioned, and conditional on license approval and validation. For venue events and raw EOD facts, a directly licensed primary-venue record is preferred; issuer filings are preferred for issuer-reported financial facts; a licensed index administrator series is preferred for constituents/TRI. A validated specialist provider may fill documented gaps, never silently replace a competing record.

Every competing value remains stored with source record, publication/retrieval times, semantics, confidence, validation, and policy. Precedence selects a publishable candidate only after semantic comparability and reconciliation. Ambiguous identity, critical field conflict, unexplained action terms, or material price/filing disagreement quarantines and escalates to Yash or a named data reviewer.
