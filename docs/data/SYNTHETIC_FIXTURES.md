# Synthetic Fixtures

`SyntheticProvider` is deterministic and offline. It includes impossible normal, rename, delisted and financial-sector entities; split, bonus, cash-dividend, symbol-change and delisting actions; a conflicting split; an explicitly unsupported rights issue; original/restated filings; after-cutoff visibility; duplicate action evidence; and future-record rejection. Provider conflict, stale price, missing identity and unsupported actions are exercised through typed validation/adversarial construction.

All identifiers/names contain `SYNTHETIC`; currency and jurisdiction values are intentionally impossible. These records are not market facts or investment evidence.
