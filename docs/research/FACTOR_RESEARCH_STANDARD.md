# Factor Research Standard

## Feature contract

Before implementation each feature declares: hypothesis/rationale; formula; inputs and units; fiscal/market time semantics; sector applicability; denominator and sign policy; missing/zero/negative behavior; publication lag; revision policy; outlier/winsorization/neutralization; source rights; golden examples; invariants; version owner.

Facts, estimates and model opinions remain distinct. Financial firms are excluded from industrial-company formulas until sector contracts exist. Forward estimates, governance narratives, news and macro vintages are excluded unless licensed and PIT-correct.

## Baseline and evidence

Begin with transparent ranks/aggregation; hard exclusions run first and cannot be offset. Weights and thresholds are versioned research proposals, never “optimal” defaults. Phase 1 validates decomposability and correctness, not return efficacy or probabilities.

For later efficacy research, preregister primary endpoint/horizon, benchmark and failure criterion; test sector/regime stability, redundancy, monotonicity, turnover/costs, missingness selection, parameter sensitivity and multiple-hypothesis risk. Separate factor efficacy, simulated strategy performance and suitability/portfolio evidence.

Any probability requires a defined target, horizon, benchmark hurdle, costs, calibration method and out-of-sample evidence. Confidence must expose its components and may only reduce actionability; it cannot bypass an exclusion.
