# Capital Protection Policy

## Non-negotiable boundary

No leverage, margin, derivatives, intraday trading, short selling, forced purchase, autonomous order, or score override of a hard stop. The system may always do nothing or abstain. Price decline alone is never a reason to add; price movement prompts thesis review.

## Control hierarchy

1. Legal/product boundary.
2. Signed, versioned suitability and capacity-for-loss profile (Phase 3).
3. Data/governance hard stops.
4. Tradability/liquidity.
5. Single-security and sector limits.
6. Factor/correlation concentration.
7. Scenario-loss/risk budget.
8. Turnover/tax review.
9. Human review and manual external order.

The spec’s 5% stock, 20% sector, 10% cash and other values are unvalidated examples, not defaults. Human confirmation is mandatory before personalized allocation.

## Invariants

A proposal cannot breach suitability, cash, stock, sector, liquidity, risk or critical-data gates. An unavailable/expired suitability profile prevents personalized action. Increasing an isolated order cannot reduce concentration/liquidity risk. Every proposal shows pre/post exposures, stressed loss, liquidity horizon, cash, constraint headroom, assumptions and evidence.

Liquidity considers stressed traded value, conservative participation, days to exit, gaps and untradeable holdings. Stress combines market decline, correlation convergence, liquidity evaporation and suspension; scenarios are not forecasts. Breaches block new exposure and trigger review—not automatic liquidation.

## Deferred human inputs

Emergency fund, debts, dependents, near-term withdrawals, income stability, household exposures, capacity for loss, horizon, tax residency, restrictions, acceptable drawdown, cash reserve and numerical limits. These are financially consequential and cannot be inferred.
